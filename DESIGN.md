---

# Design Philosophy

CitiesWalk helps people explore Kuala Lumpur while encouraging sustainable travel and healthier lifestyles.

The application should feel:

• Natural
• Fresh
• Calm
• Modern
• Friendly
• Premium
• Trustworthy

Users should immediately associate the interface with nature, walking, environmental awareness, and exploration.

The UI should never feel like:

- Banking application
- Corporate dashboard
- Heavy enterprise software
- Gaming interface
- Neon cyberpunk
- E-commerce marketplace

Instead, imagine a combination of:

- AllTrails
- Apple Health
- Google Maps
- Forest App
- Strava

"I want to explore."
